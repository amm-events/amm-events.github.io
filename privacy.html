Privacy Notice

This page explains how AMM Events intends to handle personal information received through enquiries.

Who is responsible for your information?

AMM Events is operated by Marius Dumitrascu as a sole trader. Contact: amm-events@protonmail.com or 07961 838311.

What information may be collected?

If you contact us, you may provide your name, telephone number, journey details, pick-up and destination, travel date/time and any additional details you choose to include.

How will information be used?

Information is intended to be used to respond to your enquiry, discuss availability, arrange and administer a booking, communicate about the journey, and meet relevant legal or licensing obligations.

How is information submitted through this website?

The current enquiry form opens your own email application and does not send information to or store it on this website. If you send an email, your email provider and our email service will process that message.

Sharing and retention

Personal information should only be shared where necessary to arrange the service, meet legal requirements or protect legal rights. Keep booking records only for as long as required by applicable legal, tax, insurance and licensing requirements, and securely delete information when no longer needed.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict use of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. You can contact us using the details above.
