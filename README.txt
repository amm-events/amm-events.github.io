Preview:

Quote form:
The form opens the visitor's default email app addressed to info@amm-taxi.co.uk. It does not store enquiries, send emails automatically, or confirm bookings. A server-backed form would require an additional service or hosting configuration.

Before publishing:
1. Review and personalise privacy.html and terms.html; they are templates, not legal advice.
2. Confirm all business details and the service descriptions.
3. Replace the cropped mockup car image with a real photo of your exact car if desired.
4. Check whether you need to pay the ICO data protection fee and add any third-party tools/cookie notice if applicable.
5. Ensure service and licence claims accurately reflect your current authorisations.

Upload:
Upload index.html, styles.css, script.js, privacy.html, terms.html and the complete assets folder to the hosting provider's website root. Do not remove existing IONOS email DNS records when connecting the domain.
