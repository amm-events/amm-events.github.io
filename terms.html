Booking Information

Enquiries and confirmation

Sending an enquiry does not create a confirmed booking. A journey is only confirmed when AMM Taxi & Events has replied and the journey details and fare have been agreed.

Information to provide

Please provide an accurate pick-up location, destination, date, requested time, passenger count and relevant event details. Let us know about any requirements that may affect the journey before confirmation.

Fares and payment

The fare and payment arrangements should be agreed before travel. Do not assume that a quote has been accepted or that a booking is confirmed until you receive confirmation from AMM Events.

Changes and cancellations

Contact us as soon as possible if your plans change. Cancellation can only be done with a 48 hour notice, otherwise a 30% fee will be charged.

Delays and availability

All journeys are subject to availability and confirmation. If timings or locations change, contact us so that arrangements can be discussed.

Complaints or questions
