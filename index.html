Ask us a question →
Do I need to book in advance?

AMM only handles pre-booked journeys. Contact us with your date, time, pick-up point and destination so we can check availability.

How do I book a service?

Call us or complete the enquiry form with as much journey information as you can. Your booking is not confirmed until we reply and agree the details.

Do you provide transport for events?

Yes, you can enquire about transport for weddings, parties, concerts, and other special occasions. Please share the event time and locations.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based around Weymouth and cover nearby Dorset locations, with longer-distance journeys considered by arrangement.

Does sending the booking form confirm my booking?

No. The form prepares an email enquiry. Your journey is only confirmed after AMM Events replies and agrees the details with you.

How many passengers can be transported at a time?

A maximum of 4 passengers can be transported at a time.
